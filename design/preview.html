How it works

  1. 01

    Get a code

    Press Send files. You get a six-character code and a QR to scan.

  2. 02

    Join with the code

    Scan the QR or type the code. The two devices find each other.

  3. 03

    Files go direct

    Pick what to send. It travels straight between the two devices.

Good to know

  • Encrypted

    Every transfer is encrypted between the two devices.

  • Nothing stored

    Files never pass through or sit on a server.

  • No account

    No sign-up, no email, and no app to install.

  • No size cap

    Send one photo or a whole 40 GB trip.

Files go device to device and never touch our servers.
